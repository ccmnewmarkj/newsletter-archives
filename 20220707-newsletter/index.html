Congratulations to Graciela Mochkofsky, New J-School Dean!
We are so thrilled that CCM’s executive director, Graciela Mochkofsky, has been appointed the third dean of Newmark J-School, succeeding Sarah Bartlett, who retired in June 2022. Graciela has been executive director of CCM since 2019, and founded the Latino Media Initiative in 2017 while serving as founding director of the country’s first bilingual Spanish-English master’s degree program in journalism, at Newmark J-School. Read more about our wonderful incoming dean here.
Under her leadership, CCM expanded to become a leading national resource on and for community-based local journalism for and by immigrants, people of color, and other communities that are underserved in local media across the U.S. Since 2019, CCM has generated maps and directories of multiple regional and national community media ecosystems; produced groundbreaking original research; piloted innovative new sustainability and civic engagement initiatives; launched the Black Media Initiative and is now preparing to launch an Asian Media Initiative this coming fall.

We have continued to offer free training and community spaces, public events, special reports and newsletters, and specialized fellowships and coaching to connect, support, amplify, and sustain community media. With Graciela’s continued support and advocacy for community media in her new role, we are excited to expand our vital work to strengthen and grow the national community media sector.

Please join us in congratulating Graciela on her new chapter!

Community Media Covers the NY State Primaries



As New York prepares for yet another primary election day on August 23, CCM’s 2022 New York State Elections Reporting Fellows are keeping their communities informed. So far, the 30 fellows reporting in nine languages across New York State have published 58 in-depth articles and explainers. 

The morning of the first primary election day on June 28, BKLYNER published a voter’s guide and NYCPolitics covered the consequential face-off in Brooklyn’s 60th Assembly district. The next day, Black-owned Ark Republic published a report on whether the latest round of redistricting improved the chances of former police officers turned political hopefuls.

We will share more excellent community media primary elections coverage in our September 1 newsletter, as the country gears up for the federal midterm elections on November 8.
