For community media, accuracy and digital security are more than just technical standards—they are the foundation of the trust you’ve built with your audience. This week, our Community Media Bulletin features our upcoming sessions on these areas: one on an AI tool that helps detect disinformation across Spanish-language radio stations and another on hardening digital defenses, as well as other training, events, and professional development opportunities.

As always, if you have a resource, read, or opportunity you’d like to see highlighted here, please send it our way. Email Newsletter Editor LaMonte Guillory at lamonte.guillory@journalism.cuny.edu.

CCM Highlights

Today, Jan. 29 – The AI Vanguard: VERDAD Information Session

  • The Host: Center for Community Media (CCM), featuring Martina Guzman, founder of VERDAD, and investigative journalist Tamoa Calzadilla
  • The Blueprint: This 90-minute session will dive into VERDAD (Verifying and Exposing Radio Disinformation and Discourse), an AI tool that helps journalists track and investigate misinformation on Spanish-language and ethnic radio stations—platforms often overlooked by traditional monitoring.
  • The Gain: You will learn how to use AI-driven tracking to protect your audience from targeted disinformation. While this tool is currently available only in Spanish, it will soon be accessible in other languages. Journalists interested in innovative AI strategies to combat misinformation, particularly in community news media, will benefit from attending this session. 
  • ​​​​​Register for the CCM information session on “Understanding VERDAD” - TODAY, Jan. 29, 2 pm ET / 1 pm CT / 11 am PT.

Feb. 5 – Securing the Digital Border: Cyber Safety for Journalists

  • The Host: Center for Community Media (CCM), featuring Danielle Meah, Deputy Chief Information Security Officer at Hearst
  • The Blueprint: A 90-minute workshop focused on the digital threats facing newsrooms, including hacking, phishing, and doxing. This training provides a tactical guide for protecting your online presence.
  • The Gain: By securing your digital newsroom, you combat security lapses that could compromise your safety and the trust you’ve built with your community.
  • Register for the CCM training on “Digital Safety and Best Practices for Journalists” - Feb. 5, 1 pm ET / 12 pm CT / 10 am PT. (Note: If you already registered in late 2025, your seat is secured.)

One Good Read

“The Fog of Good Intentions”: Helping Newsrooms Navigate Support

  • The Author: Yoni Greenbaum, Vice President of Product Strategy at the American Press Institute
  • The Blueprint: An essay that looks at why the journalism support world—full of mini-grants, cohorts, trainings, and fellowships—often feels like a "jumbled mess" to busy newsroom leaders. Greenbaum suggests we need a better "dispatch system" to help journalists find precisely what they need without the noise.
  • The Gain: A clearer way to think about the resources available to you. Instead of feeling overwhelmed by too many options, this piece argues for a more coordinated approach that treats a newsroom’s call for help with the urgency of a triage unit.
  • Read "The Fog of Good Intentions" by Yoni Greenbaum. Does this piece resonate with you? If it does, reach out to us at lamonte.guillory@journalism.cuny.edu. 

Professional Growth Opportunities

Trainings, Courses and Webinars​​​​​​

Navigating Crime and Gun Violence Data

  • The Host: A collaboration between the Harvard Kennedy School’s Shorenstein Center on Media, Politics and Public Policy and The Journalist’s Resource.
  • The Blueprint: A free intensive session on how to find, verify, and responsibly use crime data. The training includes a deep dive into The Trace’s Gun Violence Data Hub to help reporters find hidden narratives in local and national data.
  • The Gain: Data literacy skills that help you avoid common pitfalls in crime reporting. You will gain access to reliable resources for evidence-based reporting that strengthens community safety and trust.
  • Register for the crime data webinar - Feb. 4, 12 pm ET / 11 am CT / 9 am PT

Mobile Reporting: From the Palm of Your Hand 

  • The Host: The Institute for Independent Journalists (IIJ), featuring veteran journalist and content creator Victoria Lim.
  • The Blueprint: In this free hands-on demonstration, join freelance journalist Victoria Lim and discover a wide array of apps, gear, and strategies for shooting high-quality photos and videos using a smartphone.
  • The Gain: You will walk away with a curated list of essential mobile tools and the confidence to produce professional-grade content on the go.
  • Register for the Mobile Reporting webinar - Feb. 6, 12 pm ET / 11 am CT / 9 am PT

Listening Post Collective: Information Session

  • The Host: Listening Post Collective (LPC), an organization that helps newsrooms build deeper connections with their audiences through civic engagement.
  • The Blueprint: A free information session on how to access small grants from LPC, which will help your newsroom learn how to conduct a community information needs assessment. These assessments build trust and allow information needs to drive the reporting process. 
  • The Gain: A practical understanding of what working with LPC on an info needs assessment means, and application questions answered.
  • Register for the LPC Info Session - Feb. 9, 12 pm ET / 11 am CT / 9 am PT

Data Mindset for Editors

  • The Host: Knight Center for Journalism in the Americas at the University of Texas at Austin.
  • The Blueprint: A free, self-paced online course specifically tailored for newsroom leaders and editors. This training focuses on learning to identify data-driven story ideas, manage data-intensive projects, and guide reporters through the technical aspects of data storytelling. The course runs Feb. 9 through March 8.
  • The Gain: The ability to lead a data-literate newsroom. Rather than needing to be a data scientist yourself, you will gain the "editorial eye" needed to ensure your newsroom's data work is accurate, ethical, and impactful.
  • Register for the Data Mindset for Editors course

Covering Immigration Safely and with Care

  • The Host: National Press Club Journalism Institute, featuring a panel of national reporters from The Washington Post, The Bulwark, MPR News, and LA Public Press.
  • The Blueprint: A discussion on navigating the physical and digital threats inherent in covering immigration. The session covers risk assessment for on-the-ground reporting, from protests to enforcement actions.
  • The Gain: Specific guidance on maintaining digital security and source protection while reporting on sensitive topics. You will gain a practical guide for your own field safety kit.
  • Register for the Covering Immigration Safely and with Care panel - Feb. 10, 12 pm ET / 11 am CT / 9 am PT

Covering Climate Now Academy

  • The Host: Covering Climate Now, a global journalism collaboration dedicated to more and better coverage of the climate story.
  • The Blueprint: A free, live, online training program series of trainings and resources designed to help journalists on any beat—not just science reporters—understand and report on climate change and its local impacts. The program, which starts March 4 and runs through June 17, includes 12 weekly sessions and three community activities.
  • The Gain: Practical knowledge on how to connect global climate trends to local community concerns. You will gain access to expert-led sessions that demystify climate data and provide story ideas that resonate with your audience.
  • Apply for the Covering Climate Now Academy by Feb. 16

Beyond the Byline: Leveraging Events to Forge Community

  • The Host: Online News Association (ONA), the world’s largest organization of digital journalists.
  • The Blueprint: An interactive session focused on how newsrooms can use virtual and in-person events to deepen community engagement and create new revenue streams.
  • The Gain: A toolkit for turning your reporting into a community experience. You will learn how to plan events that resonate with your audience, build stronger relationships with your readers, and diversify your newsroom's financial support.
  • Register for the ONA community events session - Feb. 18, 2 pm ET / 1 pm CT / 11 am PT

News Creator Field Guide: Forging New Paths in Journalism

  • The Host: Knight Center for Journalism in the Americas at the University of Texas at Austin.
  • The Blueprint: A self-paced online course ($99) designed for independent journalists and news entrepreneurs. It provides a step-by-step roadmap for launching and sustaining a news project in today’s media economy. The course runs Feb. 18 through March 20.

  • Register for the News Creator Field Guide

Additional Opportunities
