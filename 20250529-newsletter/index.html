Remembering Tom Robbins

Newmark J-School is saddened to announce the passing of Tom Robbins, the school’s longtime Investigative Reporter in Residence. Robbins died at 76, following a long illness. He came to the school in 2011 after a long career at New York Daily News and Village Voice and his mentorship helped support exceptional student-produced work for City Limits, WNYC, Gothamist, and THE CITY. 


News 

Newmark J-School published a new report highlighting the transformative impact of New York City’s groundbreaking legislation supporting community media outlets, and the role of CCM’s Advertising Boost Initiative, led by Darlie Gervais, in bridging implementation gaps. 

Congratulations to New York Amsterdam News Executive and Investigative Editor Damaso Reyes, who was recently named a John S. Knight Journalism Fellow at Stanford! Reyes will be heading to Palo Alto for the academic year. He was the founding editor of Blacklight, the first investigative unit of its kind at a legacy Black newspaper and the recipient of the 2024 National Association of Black Journalists Ida B. Wells Award. 

The Haitian Times marked its 25th anniversary by partnering with the Queens Public Library, which will digitize the newspaper’s print archives and assets and showcase them in a library exhibit this fall that is part of the Queens Memory Project. The newspaper went all-digital in 2012. 

Local news startup Lookout Local launched its second bureau in Eugene, Oregon a month ago and it is already the second-largest local newsroom in the state and boasts nearly 1,000 paid members. The original bureau is still located in Santa Cruz, California and won the 2024 Pulitzer Prize in Breaking News.

Fellowships & Opportunities

Omidyar Network is taking applications for its Reporters in Residence program. This program will fund a group of freelance journalists’ deep, nuanced reporting on a variety of stories at the intersection of tech, the economy, and policy. The Summer 2025 program will run from July to December of 2025. The deadline to apply is May 30.

The 19th seeks applications for the 2025-26 Product Track cohort of the Frances Ellen Watkins Harper Fellowship. Applications for other tracks are closed. Alums of Historically Black Colleges and Universities who are early to mid-career are awarded one-year, salaried positions in reporting, audience engagement, and product management with the news outlet. Apply by May 30. 


ProPublica is taking applications for its David Burnham-TRAC Data Reporting Fellowship. This is a new fellowship, meant to provide early career data journalists the opportunity to learn from ProPublica staff and to give them the space to shed light on both the inner workings of the government and impacts of federal policy. The fellowship honors Burnham, an investigative journalist who reported on local, state and federal enforcement corruption for 50 years. He also helped found the Transactional Records Access Clearinghouse (TRAC) a data gathering, research and distribution organization at Syracuse University. The deadline is June 16.
