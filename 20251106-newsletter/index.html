✨CCM Highlights✨ 

 📢CCM is partnering with the Lenfest Institute for Journalism to spread the word to community media about the Institute’s 2025 Beyond Print Grant Program. 

This program has three components. There is $400,000 in total funding to help independent local newspapers transition from print to sustainable digital operations. Grants of up to $50,000 per organization will support projects that advance digital transformation, diversify revenue, and engage younger audiences through new platforms like podcasts, newsletters, and social media. The deadline to apply is Monday, November 17. 

The program is focused on print and print-first outlets. Newsrooms with a regularly published print product and annual revenues of at least $500,000 are eligible to apply for the grant. Eligible projects must align with one or more focus areas – ranging from digital product development and customer engagement to newsroom workflow modernization and event-based revenue. 

Some more notes on eligibility: 

  • Must be independent and local (state, city, town, hyperlocal).
  • Must be currently publishing. Organizations that once printed but have since stopped are not eligible. 
  • Must align with program focus areas (scroll down to “Eligible focus areas”). The project does not specifically have to be about print reduction, but it should focus on strengthening products to appeal to digital consumers/audiences or drive digital revenue priorities. 
  • Can be a print magazine as long as it meets the other criteria.

There are also two other offerings that publishers can take advantage of: 

  • Beyond Print Community of Practice membership: This includes a free peer-to-peer learning cohort beginning in November. The cohort will feature virtual workshops, case studies, information exchange, and a chance to network with other publishers. 
  • 1:1 intensive advisory support: Fifty publishers will have the opportunity to receive free, personalized mentoring and support to transform their print newsroom into a sustainable, digital one through the Lenfest Expert Network.

🕵️‍♂️ CCM is also hosting a training on Smart Fact-Checking: Tools, Techniques, and AI Methods for Journalists on Thursday, November 20 at 2 pm ET. The session will equip community media journalists with practical tools to combat misinformation and strengthen digital investigation skills. Through hands-on fact-checking exercises and advanced verification methods, participants will learn to authenticate sources, verify online content, and navigate AI-driven manipulation. 📰Community Media Minute🌎

What's happening in the community media world

📷 Amber Sherman, a Memphis-based creator, partnered with MLK50 as a creator-in-residence to produce civic explainer videos rooted in community engagement. Her collaboration led to a 664% rise in MLK50’s social interactions by combining her trusted, platform-native storytelling with the newsroom’s editorial rigor. MLK50’s executive editorial director Adrienne Johnson Martin defined guardrails to ensure Sherman’s content aligned with the outlet’s mission and values. This model shows how creators and community-serving newsrooms can bridge communities and journalism, expanding reach without compromising credibility.

📖One Good Read📒

Useful studies and reports to include in your week's reading

🖥️The American Journalism Project published its Field Guide: AI for Local Reporting resource. AJP’s Product & AI Studio created this guide to help local newsrooms evaluate and use AI tools for covering public meetings. It outlines what each tool does, how it’s used, who uses it, and why it matters, with a focus on transcription, summarization, and pattern recognition. The guide encourages experimentation, noting that generative AI can shift newsroom labor from manual tasks to strategic editorial work, particularly for resource-strapped community media newsrooms. Based on research, interviews, product testing, and feedback from AJP partners and experts, it compiles vendors serving both immediate and long-term newsroom needs. The resource will be routinely updated with amended and new information for publishers to access so they can experiment with various tools in order to see which works best for their newsroom. 

🗣️Pro Tip🗒️

A tip or tool for your newsroom

📊Several federal datasets have been manipulated or removed entirely from public view since the Trump administration took office in January. The Journalist’s Resource spoke to three data experts on how not having access to accurate data could affect your community, specifically the American Community Survey from the US Census Bureau, the Department of Education’s Civil Rights Data Collection, which can help parents find school districts with suitable disability resources, and the Environmental Protection Agency’s Safe Drinking Water Information System. There are also reporting tips on how to spot manipulated data, find local angles in stories about federal data, vet trustworthy sources and experts, and how to communicate to audiences about the data and the impact of data manipulation or removal.

🧑🏽‍🏫Fellowships & Opportunities✒️

ProPublica is taking applications for its Photojournalism Fellowship. The NYC-based, two-year program is open to anyone with at least three years of professional photojournalism experience, as a staffer or freelancer. The Fellow will report to the senior editor of visual storytelling and work most closely with visual editors and reporters. The deadline to apply is December 1. 

Ohio University is accepting applications for its 2026 Kiplinger Fellowship. The focus this year will be on ‘Saving Local News: A Practical Guide’ and will be held at the Scripps College of Communication on campus in Athens, Ohio, between May 11-14, 2026. The deadline to apply is December 7.

The National Press Foundation is accepting applications for its Widening the Pipeline Fellowship, which focuses on helping journalists of color with seven or less years of experience stay in and lead newsrooms around the country. The yearlong program involves virtual workshops, in-person training in Washington, DC, and one-on-one mentorship with experienced industry professionals. The deadline to apply is December 14.
