NY State Elections Fellows Report
The 2022 election season is well underway, and community media journalists are furiously covering the incredibly high-stakes statewide and federal midterm elections across New York state. Through CCM’s 2022 NY State Elections Reporting Fellowship, 30 journalists reporting from within Black, Chinese, Haitian, Nepali, West African, Spanish-speaking, Russian-speaking, local communities and more, are serving news audiences that rarely see elections coverage that matters to them in local and national news.
 

Photo by Richard Heaton for Newmark J-School

In March, Kharkiv, Ukraine-born Marina Lagunova explored national and New York state candidates who have changed political parties, for Russian-language publication Reklama. In a forthcoming issue, she will recount language and other barriers Russian-speaking voters are encountering at polling stations in Brooklyn, Queens, and the Bronx.

In April, Baruch Lytle wrote an in-depth profile of Congressman Ritchie Torres, a progressive who has remained a staunch supporter of Israel, for The Jewish Press. Later in the month, Ariama C. Long examined the impact redistricting has had on New York City’s Asian, Latinx, and Republican communities for Black-owned Amsterdam News. For the Bronx’s Riverdale Press, Sachi McClendon reported in April on uncharacteristically competitive lower-level Democratic races,

AJ Woodson kicked off May’s coverage with a report on a state appeals court ruling striking down proposed redistricting maps, and informing readers of Black-owned Black Westchester Magazine that primary elections had been delayed until August. McClendon covered allegations of fraud in the Republican primary race to contest Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez’s seat in the U.S. Congress in last week's Riverdale Press.

Meet all 30 of CCM’s NY State Elections Reporting Fellows here, and be sure to follow along with us on Twitter, where we’ll be sharing their coverage with #NYSElectionsReportingFellows.

Meet Pulitzer-Prize Winning Futuro Studios Team at #LMS22!

We couldn’t be more thrilled to join the incredible team at Futuro Studios in celebrating its well-deserved win of a Pulitzer Prize for Audio Reporting for its seven-part podcast series Suave, on a criminal justice system that sentences juveniles – largely young men of color – to life in prison.

You can celebrate them and dozens of other incredible projects and teams at the 2022 Latino Media Summit! Futuro Studios executive editor Maria Elena Garcia, creator and host of the Anything for Selena podcast, will be kicking off this year’s summit in conversation with Radio Ambulante’s Carolina Guerrera, LWC Studios’ Paulina Velasco, and La Brega host and producer Alana Casanova-Burgess, on The Rise of Bilingual Podcasting.

Read more about this and all the other public conversations, feature presentations, lightning talks, workshops, and think-in sessions on the #LMS22 agenda. And be sure to reserve your spot for the two-day summit, June 3-4, here.
