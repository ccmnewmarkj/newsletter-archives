Black Media Product Strategy Cohort Launches

On March 16, 1827, a group of free Black leaders and scholars in New York City launched Freedom’s Journal, the first Black-owned newspaper in the U.S. This week, on the 195th anniversary of this Black media founders’ day, we launched the very first Black Media Product Strategy cohort, with teams from: Ark Republic, BK Reader, Fun Times Magazine, Harvey World Herald, Houston Defender Network, Northend Agents Newspaper, OkayAfrica, The Atlanta Voice, The Black Wall Street Times, The Community Voice, The Haitian Times, and The San Diego Voice & Viewpoint Newspaper. 

​​​​​A collaboration between Newmark J-School’s J+ and CCM, this six-month-long, tuition-free program will train teams from Black-owned newsrooms to build product strategies for digital transformation, audience growth and sustainability. Read more about the program, the coaches, and the participants here.

The 12 newsrooms selected to participate in this inaugural cohort reflect the true range and diversity of Black-owned media. They report from 10 U.S. states in every region, and serve hyperlocal neighborhoods, African American audiences, and Black immigrant communities from across Africa and the Caribbean. They are legacy newsrooms and new digital startups, and their leaders are longtime journalists or entrepreneurs in their second or third careers, some second-generation owners and others are new to Black media.
