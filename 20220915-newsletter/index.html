Asian Media Initiative Launches
We are excited to announce the launch of the Asian Media Initiative at the Center for Community Media. It is the first national effort to organize and build both visibility and sustainability for community media for and by Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders who are among the fastest growing populations in the country.

From the first Chinese-language newspaper in the U.S., The Golden Hills’ News (金山日新錄) which began publishing in San Francisco in 1854, to more recently established outlets like The Yappie and American Kahani, the Asian Media Initiative seeks to support growth, storytelling capabilities, and journalistic innovation in these outlets and communities.

CCM’s own Kavitha Rajagopalan will be the initiative’s inaugural director, bringing with her enthusiasm for and commitment to the mission and deep experience in building partnerships with Black, Latino, and Asian community media.
We hope you can join us for the virtual launch event on Thursday, September 15, 2022 from 4-5:45pm EST. Asian media journalists Rong Xiaoqing (榮筱箐) of Sing Tao Daily, Kishor Panthi of Khasokhas and NepYork, Indah Nuritasari of Indonesian Lantern, and Giannina Ong of Mochi Magazine will be on hand to share their invaluable insights about community media. Following welcoming remarks by Newmark J-School dean Graciela Mochkofsky, Rajagopalan will then moderate a panel discussion on the history of legacy Asian community media and digital storytelling, featuring CCM communications director and longtime community media advocate Jehangir Khattak, and digital storytelling pioneer Dr. Shirley Suet-ling Tang (鄧雪齡).

This Fall at CCM

We have an exciting semester ahead of us at CCM. Not only is the Asian Media Initiative launching but there is a new pilot program to mentor NYC newsrooms in digital marketing.

There will be two tracks in digital analytics mentorship as well as a program to help a select group of publishers develop media kits for their outlets.

CCM’s Black Media Initiative is also working on ways to support new revenue models for community media outlets through an Audience Revenue Cohort. Stay tuned to this space for announcements on when the application for that program will be open.
