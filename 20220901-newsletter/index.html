CCM Receives $2 Million to Support Community Media

We are thrilled to share that CCM has received an investment of $2.01 million to expand and deepen our work with the national community media sector, from the John S. and James L. Knight Foundation. The grant renews an initial $900,000 investment to support our 2019 re-launch as a national resource and advocacy center for community media, and is the largest single investment in CCM’s history.

With this support, CCM will expand our incredibly successful Advertising Boost Initiative beyond New York City to support other community media networks and ecosystems across the U.S. The Advertising Boost Initiative supported New York City’s efforts to deliver more than $25 million in city government advertising dollars to community media between 2020 and 2022. Read our 2020 independent report and our 2022 report update for more information.

This renewed investment will enable CCM to continue serving community media publishers through the Black Media Initiative, Latino Media Initiative, and forthcoming Asian Media Initiative, and grow our programming for journalists, leaders, and creators in the national community media ecosystem. Read the announcement here. 

If you’d like to help us in our critical mission to sustain and grow the national community media sector, apply today to be CCM’s new executive director!

Black Media Initiative Members Launch News Channel



A brand-new Black media-led news channel, Black Headline News, launched on August 24. Bringing together original reporting from 13 Black-owned newsrooms across the country whose leaders boast more than 70 years of combined experience in Black media, the 24-7 news channel features news coverage, sports, commentary, docuseries, and more. The founders are members of Black News Link, a network of Black media journalists. The Black Media Initiative serves as a backbone support organization to the collective.
"The idea for Black Headline News rose from an initial networking session the Black Media Initiative hosted,” said Black Media Initiative director Cheryl Thompson-Morton. “That initial meeting, and the conversation about building the collective power of Black media birthed the Black News Link, a peer support group for Black news leaders to share stories, give and receive advice, and raise the profile of the entire sector. This moment shows what can happen when we are able to bring people together."

Read more about the launch, and visit Black Headline News for more information on how to watch the channel.

Announcing the 2023 CCM Fellow in News Innovation and Leadership

On August 15, CCM and J+ announced that Rebecca Klein, general manager of New York Focus, had been named the second annual CCM Fellow in News Innovation and Leadership. Rebecca, who teaches journalism at NYU, was previously a senior reporter at Huffington Post. New York Focus was launched in October 2020 to make the notoriously opaque New York State government and legislative processes more transparent to local news audiences. Read our tweet, and this announcement on the new cohort from J+. ​​
The Executive Program in News Innovation and Leadership, now in its third year, will support 23 incredible media leaders, several of them leading community media outlets for immigrants and people of color in the U.S. The yearlong hybrid program that kicks off in September will have participants work on strategic capstone projects that tackle larger issues in the industry and their newsrooms. Read more about the program and the 2023 cohort here.
