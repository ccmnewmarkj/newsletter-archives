Jean Friedman-Rudovsky is our new Executive Director

Dear CCM Community,

I'm thrilled to share exciting news about the future of the Center for Community Media. Today, The Craig Newmark Graduate School of Journalism announced the appointment of Jean Friedman-Rudovsky as Executive Director of CCM, effective August 1, 2025.

This appointment comes at a pivotal moment for community journalism, when local outlets serve as the most trusted sources of information amid widespread disinformation challenges. Jean brings exactly the kind of nationally recognized expertise in collaborative journalism that our field needs right now.

What makes Jean the perfect fit for CCM? Her track record speaks for itself. She co-founded Resolve Philly in 2018, where she pioneered collaborative journalism practices that have been replicated globally. Under her leadership, Resolve became one of the most highly regarded young news organizations in the nation, demonstrating how community-responsive journalism can drive both local impact and broader change. She successfully raised $13 million over eight years while building a workplace centered on values, equity, and intentional practices.

But Jean's expertise extends far beyond organizational leadership. She has trained, consulted, or coached more than 50 newsroom leaders in collaboration, management, equitable workplace practices, fundraising, and community engagement. This hands-on experience will allow CCM to expand its ability to provide essential tools, training, and resources to under-resourced community media outlets nationwide.

Her work has consistently focused on elevating voices from communities of color and amplifying stories and perspectives that are often overlooked by traditional media. Her collaborative approach gained national attention through the Reentry Project, a groundbreaking media collaboration she organized in Philadelphia that changed policy and practice around prisoner reentry.

Having had the privilege of serving as interim executive director, I've seen firsthand how essential CCM's work is to the broader journalism ecosystem. More than that, I've had the honor of meeting many of you and supporting your vital work over this past year. I deeply believe in your mission to serve communities whose voices and stories are too often overlooked, and I've been inspired by your commitment to trusted, community-rooted journalism.

Jean's appointment ensures that the Center will continue to serve as a national hub of information, resources, and training for news organizations that provide essential local coverage for populations whose voices and issues are underrepresented in mainstream media. I'm excited to continue working with the CCM team to identify the best ways to connect the work of the Engagement Journalism program with yours, strengthening the bonds between academic training and community practice.

I'm confident that under Jean's leadership, CCM will continue to support journalism that is both locally rooted and nationally relevant, serving community needs while contributing to broader democratic discourse.

Please join me in welcoming Jean to our community. I look forward to seeing how she'll help build the future of community media and create the newsrooms our communities deserve.

Warmly,
Carla Zanoni,  Director of the Engagement Journalism M.A. Program
