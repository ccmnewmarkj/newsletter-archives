The 2022 Latino Media Summit Returns

The 2022 Latino Media Summit website is live! Visit today to find a full list of more than 40 brilliant Latinx thinkers, creators, journalists and leaders, who will come together for two days of celebration and community in New York City on June 3 and 4, 2022.

This year, the summit will take place in person, with the option to live-stream sessions, at The Clemente Soto Vélez Cultural and Educational Center (The Clemente), a premiere Puerto Rican and Latinx cultural institution in a beautiful historic landmark building on New York City’s Lower East Side. Teatro SEA, a leading bilingual arts-in-education organization and children’s theater, also resides in The Clemente and will also be hosting select LMS sessions.

As in previous years, the 2022 LMS will be bilingual in both Spanish and English, with sessions in a dynamic array of formats designed to enrich the experiences of both our in-person and virtual audiences. Attendees will be able to hear from influential thinkers in feature presentations and public conversations; discover innovative projects in Latino media in lightning talks; pre-register to build skills, brainstorm, and make important connections in intimate workshops and think-in sessions; find inspiration at live performances and documentary screenings; and network over cafecito with each other, as well as with potential grantmakers and mentors.

The 2022 LMS is made possible with generous support from our sponsors: The Google News Initiative, Telemundo, The Pulitzer Center, Univision, Spectrum / Charter Communications, and Con Edison, along with The Clemente and Teatro SEA. Registrations will open on Tuesday, April 19, when we will also release the complete summit agenda. Meantime, be sure to follow the Latino Media Summit on Twitter, Facebook, and Instagram for summit news and updates.
Asian Media Women’s Network

As women journalists in Asian media continue to report on – and inhabit – the front lines of the ongoing hate crimes crisis, CCM is preparing to convene an Asian Media Women’s Network. The network will offer a supportive space for women journalists reporting within and for diverse Asian American communities across the U.S., as they navigate misogyny, online harassment, and limited resources to deliver essential in-language and culturally relevant journalism to Asian American and Pacific Islander news audiences. If you would like to participate in the network, please email Kavitha Rajagopalan.

Last week, we hosted a public conversation between women journalists in Asian media, sharing their experiences and strategies for covering hate crimes in their communities. You can find a recording of this conversation here. Please request permission before quoting the panelists or participants for publication.

Resources

Solutions Journalism Network is accepting applications for its Complicating the Narratives Fellowship. Fellows will receive honoraria of up to $5,000, as well as ongoing support within the Solutions Journalism Network community. Deadline to apply is April 29. Read more, find out about information sessions, and apply here.

Society of Environmental Journalists is accepting proposals for story grants on U.S. public lands. Grantees will receive up to $5,000 in stipends and expenses. Story proposals that reflect diverse and inclusive perspectives are encouraged. The deadline to apply is May 16. Read more and apply here.

The USC Annenberg Center for Health Journalism is accepting applications for its 2022 National Fellowship. Fellows will participate in a five-day fellowship (July 10-14); and receive mentorship and a $2,000-$10,000 reporting grant to complete a substantive reporting project on health disparities, vulnerable children and families, and the conditions and forces that contribute to their well-being. All admitted applicants are eligible to apply for the Community Engagement Fund, which provides supplemental funding for up to five fellows to underwrite community engagement strategies – preference is given to reporters collaborating with ethnic media outlets. Read more and submit a fellowship interest form here.
