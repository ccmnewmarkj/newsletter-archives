News 

 

Newmark J-School Alum Mike Reicher ('09) was part of the Seattle Times Pulitzer Local Reporting finalist team for 2025! 

News Futures, an initiative that serves as a space for journalists and organizations to create a clearer picture of how journalism can serve as civic infrastructure and be “a public good,” was launched this past February. Darryl Holliday, lead steward for the initiative and Newmark J-School’s Entrepreneur-in-Residence spoke to Editor & Publisher about the six years leading up to the launch and growing tenfold in the weeks since. 

Geeta Anand, Pulitzer Prize-winning journalist and most recently the Dean of the University of California Berkeley Graduate School of Journalism, was named the next Editor in Chief of VTDigger. The move is a return to the state for Anand, who had been a reporter in the 1990s for The Rutland Herald. 

Local news will soon have its own television show on NBC, in the style of the network’s ‘The Office,’ Nieman Lab reported. ‘The Paper’ airs starting in September. 

The National Trust for Local News is selling 21 local publications in the Denver area to Times Media Group (TMG), a community news publisher based in Tempe, Arizona. TMG operates more than 60 publications in Arizona and California, ranging from free weekly newspapers, magazines, and specialty publications. The National Trust will continue to run seven outlets in the state that will become part of the Colorado Trust for Local News. 

Texas Tribune has acquired community news outlet Austin Monitor. The move comes on the heels of the state news outlet’s move to create a local newsroom in Waco, the Waco Bridge. 

CherryRoad Media, founded by tech CEO Jeremy Gulban, has purchased nearly 100 community media outlets around the country thus far. Gulban, on his motivation for building the mass chain of local news outlets, told Poynter: “I felt it was unacceptable for a handful of Big Tech companies to control all the information, all the commerce in the country…The vision was that newspapers were the alternative to that, because they were local in every single county in the country."

Fellowships & Opportunities

Omidyar Network is taking applications for its Reporters in Residence program. This program will fund a group of freelance journalists’ deep, nuanced reporting on a variety of stories at the intersection of tech, the economy, and policy. The Summer 2025 program will run from July to December of 2025. The deadline to apply is May 30. 

ProPublica is taking applications for its David Burnham-TRAC Data Reporting Fellowship. This is a new fellowship, meant to provide early career data journalists the opportunity to learn from ProPublica staff and to give them the space to shed light on both the inner workings of the government and impacts of federal policy. The fellowship honors Burnham, an investigative journalist who reported on local, state and federal enforcement corruption for 50 years. He also helped found the Transactional Records Access Clearinghouse (TRAC) a data gathering, research and distribution organization at Syracuse University. The deadline is June 16.

Events

May 29 (Online): CCM hosts an event on how to cover municipal elections with Rachel Holliday Smith, Managing Editor at THE CITY. This training focuses on covering the New York City municipal elections, but we welcome journalists from all locations. Many of the insights and tips will be relevant and applicable beyond NYC.

June 5 (Online): CCM’s Asian Media Initiative hosts Community Archives: Preserving & Protecting AAPI History with Randy Gonzalez, founder of Filipino La., Naomi Ostwald Kawamura, Executive Director of Densho, and Samip Mallick, Executive Director of South Asian American Digital Archive.

May 19-20 (Detroit): Online News Association (ONA) and Newsroom Robots host the AI Leadership Summit to gather leaders and executives to discuss how AI has impacted the news industry and newsroom leadership and culture in outlets of all sizes.
