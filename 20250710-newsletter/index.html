News 

Congratulations to our Newmark J-School colleague Kyle Plantz, who is stepping into a new role as Senior Director of Leadership Programs at the school’s J+ initiative. After managing professional training programs at the school for the past four years Kyle will be leading the Executive Program in News Innovation and Leadership. Cynthia DuBose, who is Senior Vice President for Products and Experience at McClatchy, will serve as Lead Coach and an instructor for the J+ monthly Leadership Circles. J+ has been a longtime partner of CCM and most recently helped run the AI Community Engagement Lab to empower community media journalists to use AI for audience engagement, building trust, and increasing revenue.

Many smaller newsrooms lack sufficient or affordable media liability insurance, leaving them open to libel lawsuits from the community on which they report, elected officials, and businesses. Dick Tofel, through his Second Rough Draft newsletter, reported there are now solid options available for this type of insurance for cash-strapped publishers.

The National Hurricane Center urged journalists to change strategy when reporting on storms and has added revised and new tools to warn people about the size, path, and severity of storms but also other dangers like rip currents in bodies of water and post-storm heat, health issues, and access to food, water, and medical care.

L.A. Taco was originally conceived as a hyperlocal food truck and taco review site for Los Angelenos, but lately its coverage has been more about immigration and federal law enforcement as protestors took to the streets and food service workers lived in fear, The Washington Post reports.

SFGATE is the most popular local news site in the U.S., according to Press Gazette’s monthly ranking of the largest news websites. It ranked #37 out of the top 50 with 27 million visits last month and has achieved its success by filling news gaps, having a wide range of reporting on the region, and having an ad-funded model, per Press Gazette.

Fellowships & Opportunities

The New York Times is hiring its next Reporter Fellow, Local Investigations. Fellows working in local media or as freelancers will get the opportunity to investigate a story under the editorial guidance of Dean Baquet, NYT’s former executive editor and a group of veteran investigative editors. The fellows will be based in the communities where they report and make periodic trips to New York for training. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. 

Altavoz Lab is accepting applications for its 2025 Community Journalist Fellowship. The seven-month program is open to journalists working in English and Spanish-language community media and the focus for this year’s cohort will be those working in areas with low civic heath indicators, border communities in Arizona, New Mexico, and Texas, and underreported communities in Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Mississippi, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Virginia, West Virginia, Kansas, Oklahoma, Texas, Illinois, and Puerto Rico. The deadline is July 18.

Religion News Service and Interfaith America Magazine are accepting applications for its Religion Journalism Fellowship. This program’s goal is for future religion news journalists to develop their awareness of the impact of faith on politics and culture. The fellowship will run from September 2025 to May 2026. Fellows are expected to write at least one feature religion story monthly, published on the RNS website. Deadline to apply is July 18.

Events

July 9-12 (Chicago): The National Association of Hispanic Journalists hosts its annual conference. The theme this year: Stronger Together - Journalism, Community, and the Power of Truth. 

July 30-August 3 (Seattle): The Asian American Journalists Association hosts its annual conference. The programming includes panels, career development sessions, workshops, and networking sessions with regional chapters.

August 6-10 (Cleveland): The National Association of Black Journalists hosts its annual conference. The theme this year: Resilience, Advocacy, Excellence: Evolution of Our Voices.

August 18-21 (Chicago): Local Media Association hosts LMA Fest. The conference includes two tracks: C-level executives and digital sales managers. 

September 3-5 (St. Louis): Local Independent Online News (LION) Publishers hosts the Independent News Sustainability Summit, a national conference that will gather independent news publishers, industry experts, and supporters of independent news to share expertise, lessons learned, and actionable advice on running a news business for solopreneurs and others.

September 10-13 (New Orleans): The Online News Association hosts its annual conference with three themes: climate, AI, and resilience in journalism.
