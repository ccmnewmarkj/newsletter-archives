SAVE THE DATE: 2022 Latino Media Summit
The Latino Media Summit returns June 3-4, 2022! Created in 2017, the Latino Media Summit is an annual celebration of the visionary creativity and innovation in journalism for and by Latinx communities across the U.S. Through a series of public conversations, workshops, performances and networking opportunities, the Latino Media Summit brings together hundreds of Latinx journalists and creators to share strategies and ideas, and celebrate the power of Latino media.
This year, the Latino Media Summit will offer both virtual and in-person programming. We will soon be opening registrations and announcing the #LMS22 lineup. Be sure to visit the summit website for updates, and follow the Latino Media Summit on Instagram and Twitter!

BMI Exchange Sessions Bring Tips – and Community
November 3, the Black Media Initiative hosted the second of its three BMI Exchange Sessions, on External Revenue Models. In a vibrant conversation, Flint Beat founder Jiquanda Johnson, UrbanGeekz CEO Kunbi Tinuoye, and Q City Metro founder Glenn Burkins shared powerful strategies for exploring and generating new revenue streams from such avenues as sponsorships, fundraising, and more. You can find a recording of the session here.

The next and final BMI Exchange Session of the year will take place on December 7. To receive invitations to Black Media Initiative events and other CCM events and trainings, be sure to sign up for our mailing list here.

New Projects to Watch

Journalists Melissa del Bosque and Todd Miller have launched a new outlet covering the U.S.-Mexico border, The Border Chronicle. They draw on their expertise as longtime local reporters covering border communities in both Texas and Arizona to offer in-depth, community-based reportage and analysis on security, surveillance, and environmental issues on the border for national audiences. Visit and subscribe here.

Conecta Arizona, serving Spanish-speaking communities in Phoenix and on the U.S.-Mexico border living in Arizona, has launched a newsletter. Visit and subscribe here.
