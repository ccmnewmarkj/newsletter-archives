Registrations Open: 2022 Latino Media Summit!

Five years ago, Newmark J-School launched the Latino Media Summit to not just showcase the sheer breadth and depth of brilliance in media for and by Latinx communities across the U.S., but to celebrate it. Even when crammed into virtual-only squares during the pandemic, the energy and vitality of the LMS community could not be contained. Now back in-person, with most sessions live-streamed, the LMS is poised to bring together hundreds of Latinx journalists, thinkers and creators to build community, learn together, and uplift each other for two full days June 3-4.

Registrations are now open!

This year, our conversations will center on the issues most impacting Latinx news audiences and their media today: polarization, voting rights ahead of the upcoming midterm elections, and the rise of bilingual podcasting. We will touch on these themes throughout the summit. We hope you’ll join us online or in person at The Clemente Soto Vélez Cultural and Educational Center, a Puerto Rican and Latinx cultural complex inside a historic landmark building in New York City’s Lower East Side.

Participants will discover exciting projects in Latino media, like outlets that connect with vulnerable communities via SMS and combat disinformation on WhatsApp; that create Spanish-language news for long-ignored communities in North Carolina or scoop major mainstream players in Los Angeles; that celebrate Black Latinas, report from within prisons, and elevate trans Latinx experiences. They will hear from craft masters in political cartooning, photojournalism and investigative reporting, and find inspiration in live performance, bilingual stand-up comedy, and documentary film. They will learn new techniques in intimate workshops and brainstorm projects in think-in sessions, and have the chance to connect not only in courtyards and corridors between sessions, but in curated networking sessions with potential funders and mentors.

Visit the Latino Media Summit website to find the full list of more than 40 speakers and the complete summit agenda. To receive regular updates about the Summit and find out about special offers – from opportunities to share your input, pre-register for workshops, drawings for free tickets and more, subscribe to the LMS newsletter. Be sure to follow #LMS22 on Twitter, Facebook and Instagram for the latest news!

Congratulations

Congratulations to Claudia Irizarry Aponte and Josefa Velasquez, who have been nominated for a James Beard Award for their reporting on Los Deliveristas Unidos for The City. Don’t miss the chance to hear these two brilliant journalists in conversation with organizers behind the deliveristas’ labor rights movement at the 2022 Latino Media Summit!

A very warm congratulations and happy second birthday to Spanish-language Conecta Arizona, which has grown from a news source exclusively published on WhatsApp, to a daily radio show, to a newsletter (read and subscribe here!), and soon a digital news outlet. If you’re in Phoenix, Arizona, celebrate with them in person on April 30!

Congratulations to The Caribbean Voice on launching the new flipbook edition of its magazine. Find the inaugural issue here, and be sure to check out the latest issue here.

Congratulations and welcome to Documented’s three new multilingual community correspondents for immigration news, serving Chinese, Latinx and Caribbean New Yorkers, including Newmark J-School bilingual program graduate Rommel Ojeda. Meet them here.

Contribute & Engage

Dr. Gheni Platenburg, assistant professor of journalism at Auburn University is seeking Black journalists to participate in a study on the ways race impacts the lives and work of Black journalists in predominantly white newsrooms, called “The Racial Divide: Black Media Practitioners, White Newsrooms.” Interested parties can email Dr. Platenburg.

The News Leaders Association and allies have launched an open letter to The Pulitzer Prizes, encouraging them to include a requirement for all newsrooms to have participated in a survey on staff diversity in order to be eligible for an award. Your organization can sign the letter here.

PEN America has released a report on its survey of more than 1,000 reporters and editors across the U.S. on how disinformation is disrupting the practice of journalism, called Hard News: Journalists and the Threat of Disinformation. Community media journalists were invited to participate. Read it here.

The National Book Critics Circle is accepting applications for the 2022-2023 Emerging Critics Fellowship.
