It has been a few months since we last delivered the CCM Bulletin newsletter into your inbox. That doesn't mean things have slowed down behind the scenes. With summer officially upon us, things are heating up across the community media sector. We are glad to be back delivering a fresh breakdown of what is happening, what is changing, and where the momentum is building.

So, where have we been? ICYMI: On June 9th, we dropped a special CCM Spotlight Takeover, officially launching the Center for Community Media's 2026-2028 Strategic Plan. Transitioning our operational focus toward this unified framework required deep, intentional planning on our part. While this temporarily paused our usual newsletter cadence, it was necessary work to ensure we continue providing resources, insights, and tools that are useful, deliberate, and built to move our sector from isolated survival to collective power. If you haven't had a chance yet, you can read Executive Director Jean Friedman-Rudovsky’s statement. You can also review the complete strategic plan layout to see how our three pillars—Analyze, Amplify, and Activate—are structured to serve you.

In the sections that follow, we provide the research, tools, and career pathways to support your newsroom’s growth and impact.

Data & Tracking: Mapping the Ecosystem

Syracuse University & Rebuild Local News: The Local News Research Hub

  • The Blueprint: A centralized, multi-disciplinary database produced by Syracuse University and Rebuild Local News that unifies scattered academic research across fields like economics, political science, and public policy. The hub features around 170 studies, including peer-reviewed articles, dissertations, and working papers, each with vetted, easy-to-read summaries that outline key findings and practical implications.
  • The Gain: Moving past the basic argument that "journalism is good" to deploy rigorous, numerical evidence. This repository equips newsrooms, policymakers, and advocates with the hard data—such as the measurable economic and civic impacts of local news loss—needed to make a compelling, nuanced case to non-traditional funders and lawmakers for targeted support.
  • Read about the new database on Nieman Lab here.

ICE Deaths Tracker | LA Taco

  • The Blueprint: A national register and public documentation project launched by LA Taco that logs, tracks, and contextualizes deaths tied to federal immigration enforcement actions and custody since January 2025. While tracking data across the country, the tool heavily anchors its on-the-ground reporting and oversight within the Southern California region, combining official government releases with independent investigations to surface the human stories behind the data.
  • The Gain: A masterclass in editorial adaptation. This project illustrates how an independent, neighborhood-focused food and culture outlet can pivot its Southern California social capital to build a vital, public-interest investigative tool for immigrant communities.
  • Access the LA Taco ICE Deaths Tracker here.

The Local News Crisis is an Audience Crisis | The PIVOT Fund

  • The Blueprint: An editorial field essay by The PIVOT Fund exploring the shifting media landscape in Wisconsin. The piece highlights how independent, community-embedded information providers, ranging from bilingual digital networks to grassroots neighborhood organizers, are successfully reaching populations that legacy newsrooms have disconnected from.
  • The Gain: An insightful perspective on audience reality, providing a conceptual framework for media leaders and funders to rethink how trust operates outside of traditional journalism institutions, highlighting the need to resource informal information ecosystems.
  • Read the full PIVOT Fund Wisconsin study here.

Professional Growth Opportunities

2026 Indigenous Media Conference | Indigenous Journalists Association (IJA)

  • The Blueprint: The annual flagship convening of the Indigenous Journalists Association, taking place from July 22-24 in Portland, Oregon. The conference brings together Indigenous creators, reporters, and media executives to focus on narrative sovereignty, local policy coverage, and digital innovation in tribal media.
  • The Gain: This is a premier space to break the isolation of local reporting, share replicable survival models, and connect directly with a national network of media changemakers.
  • Register for the Indigenous Media Conference here.

Trauma-Informed Reporting for Mental Health | Poynter

  • The Blueprint: A specialized, on-demand webinar focused on equipping reporters and editors with the ethical and psychological tools required to cover trauma and mental health responsibly without exploiting vulnerable communities or burning out staff.
  • The Gain: Newsrooms frequently navigate deep community trauma. This course provides practical frameworks for active listening, empathetic interviewing, and protecting your team's organizational health.
  • Access the Trauma-Informed Reporting webinar recording for free here.

Writers Resistance Lab | Muslim Counterpublics Lab

  • The Blueprint: An ongoing interactive workshop and cohort space designed for writers, independent creators, and community journalists navigating systemic pushback, focusing on building editorial resistance, narrative power, and collaborative storytelling models.
  • The Gain: Participants gain direct access to peer-learning spaces and professional mentorship to elevate their community-driven commentary and advocacy writing.
  • Apply for the Writers Resistance Lab sessions here.

Trainings, Courses, and Resources

Organizing Collective Power: A How-To Series | Life is a Sacred Text

  • The Blueprint: A sharp, analytical deep-dive article series exploring the historical and structural mechanics of grassroots organizing, building collective power, and sustaining community-led social action.
  • The Gain: While marginally related to day-to-day newsroom mechanics, this series offers brilliant strategic insights for media leaders looking to position their journalism as an active utility for civic health.
  • Read the collective action series here.

Calls for Applications and Submissions

The Rory Peck Awards | Rory Peck Trust

  • The Blueprint: The premier global awards program dedicated explicitly to recognizing excellence, bravery, and impact among freelance journalists, camera operators, and independent information providers covering hard news and local crises.
  • The Gain: A massive opportunity to secure international visibility, validation, and professional recognition for independent creators who often operate without the institutional safety net of a major newsroom.
  • Submit nominations for the Rory Peck Awards here by July 10.

2026 Kari Howard Fund for Narrative Journalism | International Women’s Media Foundation (IWMF)

  • The Blueprint: An open grant program administered by the IWMF that awards up to $5,000 in direct project support to women and nonbinary print journalists with three or more years of professional experience. The fund backs deeply reported, character-driven narrative journalism projects worldwide, with a requirement that the work is reported and published in English within six months of the award.
  • The Gain: This fund offers a crucial financial runway for experienced independent creators and staff feature reporters to pursue time-intensive longform storytelling that captures everyday human experiences and illuminates critical social issues.
  • Apply for the Kari Howard Fund for Narrative Journalism here by July 12.

Open Call for Local News Innovation | Press Forward Chicago

  • The Blueprint: The newest localized funding call from Press Forward Chicago, looking to inject direct capital into independent, hyper-local newsrooms and community creators serving historically divested neighborhoods across the Chicagoland area.
  • The Gain: An exceptional pipeline to secure the resource-intensive capital needed to expand your reporting footprint or stabilize your operational infrastructure.
  • Apply for the Press Forward Chicago open call here by July 31.

Community Archiving Call: The Millner History Collection | Henry County Enterprise

  • The Blueprint: A regional call to action highlighting a massive preservation effort in the Piedmont region of Virginia, where decades of invaluable, raw African American history are seeking a permanent, protected home for public study.
  • The Gain: This is an urgent invitation for Black media organizations, community archivists, and narrative historians across Virginia and the broader Mid-Atlantic to partner, cover, and help digitize this vital piece of historical community infrastructure before it is lost.
  • Read the Henry County Enterprise archival story here.

Partner Investigations & Publishing Pipelines | Prison Journalism Project (PJP)

  • The Blueprint: An ongoing call for corporate partnerships, local newsrooms, and freelance editors to collaborate with incarcerated writers, providing editorial development, publishing platforms, and safe technical pathways to surface stories from inside the carceral state.
  • The Gain: Expand your newsroom's coverage of criminal justice and systemic inequity by integrating highly accurate, first-person reporting backed by PJP’s robust editing and verification network.
  • Learn how to work with the Prison Journalism Project here.
