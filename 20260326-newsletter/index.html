As the media landscape navigates a period of profound technological and economic change, the need for robust, community-centered journalism has never been greater. But meeting that need requires newsrooms to be well-funded, secure, and equipped with the latest skills.

This week’s bulletin is filled with resources designed to do exactly that. Below, you will find significant funding opportunities for innovative storytelling, fellowships that buy you the time to dig deep, and essential training on everything from physical safety and digital security to leveraging AI and navigating layoffs.

But first, an urgent call to action: Today, March 26, is the absolute final day to register your outlet for Local News Day. Registering ensures your newsroom is added to a national "local news finder" directory that is actively pushing to include robust language search functions. This is crucial visibility for ethnic and community media, making your work discoverable to new audiences on a national platform. Register your newsroom right here, right now.

Now, let’s dive into the rest of this week's opportunities.
